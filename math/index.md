

# Hardy–Weinberg Principle

**Allele frequencies**

- *p* — frequency of the WT (REF) allele
- *q* — frequency of the ALT allele

$$p + q = 1$$

$$(p + q)^2 = p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1$$

**Genotype frequencies**

| Term | Genotype |
|------|----------|
| $p^2$ | HOM WT |
| $2pq$ | HET |
| $q^2$ | HOM ALT |

## Applications

### Disease Prevalence ($P$)

**Recessive mode of inheritance**

Only homozygous variants express the disease:

$$P = q^2 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad q = \sqrt{P}$$

**Dominant mode of inheritance**

$$P = 2pq + q^2$$

If the disease is sufficiently rare, $q$ is tiny, so $p \approx 1$ and $q^2$ is negligible. Then $P$ can be approximated as:

$$P \approx 2q$$

$$q \approx \frac{P}{2}$$



**Example**
Take, for instance, identifying the allele frequencies for a disorder with a prevalence of $P = 1 / 1{,}000{,}000{,}000 = 10^{-9}$.

**Recessive**

$$q = \sqrt{P} = \sqrt{10^{-9}} \approx 0.0000316227766 \approx 3.16 \times 10^{-5}$$

**Dominant**

$$q \approx \frac{P}{2} = \frac{10^{-9}}{2} = 0.0000000005 = 5 \times 10^{-10}$$
